Rights of Users under GDPR

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or its equivalents, users have several rights regarding the processing of their personal data:

  • Right to Information and Access: Users have the right to be informed about how their personal data is being processed and to obtain access to their personal data held by the data controller.
  • Right to Rectification: Users can request the correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Users can request the deletion of their personal data under specific circumstances.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing: Users have the right to restrict the processing of their personal data in certain situations.
  • Right to Data Portability: Users can request to receive their personal data in a structured format.
  • Right to Object: Users have the right to object to the processing of their personal data.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: If the processing is based on consent, users have the right to withdraw it at any time.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: Users have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
